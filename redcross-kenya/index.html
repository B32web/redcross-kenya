Our Aid Tiers

Monthly Financial Support

Based on your situation, the system assigns you to one of five tiers.

Tier 1: Basic Support

KES 10,000 / mo

For individuals facing general economic hardship.

Qualification: General economic difficulty

Tier 2: Calamity Affected

KES 15,000 / mo

For households impacted by floods, drought, fire, or other natural disasters.

Qualification: Natural calamity impact

Tier 3: Disease Support

KES 20,000 / mo

For individuals living with chronic or acute disease.

Qualification: Chronic or acute illness

Tier 4: Loss of Parent

KES 25,000 / mo

For children who have lost one or both parents.

Qualification: One or both parents deceased

Tier 5: Severe Disability

KES 30,000 / mo

For individuals living with severe or moderate disability.

Qualification: Severe or moderate disability

How to Apply

Registration Process

Follow these simple steps to apply for monthly humanitarian aid.

1

Complete the Form

Fill in your personal details and answer the questionnaire.

2

Get Assessed

The system automatically places you in the appropriate tier.

3

Review Your Tier

See your assigned category and monthly aid amount.

4

Submit Your Application

Submit your application with all supporting documents.

5

Await Review

Our team reviews your application within 24‑48 hours.

6

Receive Monthly Aid

Approved applicants receive aid via M‑PESA STK Push every month.
